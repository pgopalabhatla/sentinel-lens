# Live-week protocol: a scripted second world

Written and committed BEFORE the scripted week was run. Its purpose is the same as that of
[test-protocol.md](test-protocol.md): to fix in advance what is run, how it is scored and what is
reported, so that the result cannot be shaped afterwards.

The test week (Oct 19-25 of the evaluated dataset) has been seen and is used up. This is a second
world for the same, unchanged system: a quiet generated world with no incidents (`data_live`), into
which failures are injected while it is streamed.

## Status when this was written

- The seven scenarios have NOT been run through the engine, on any data. No scenario, range, seed or
  rule was chosen by looking at what the detectors do with it.
- What has been run: unit tests of the injector on a synthetic stream (readings only); the scheduled
  week through the injector WITHOUT the engine, to check that every injection starts and stops as
  scheduled and that the stream is untouched outside the injections (readings only); and a rehearsal
  of the whole pipeline with a dummy injection that is not one of the seven (one restart of
  `airflow-worker-0`, on twelve hours of the train split of the evaluated dataset).
- The quiet week with nothing injected is known (docs/log.md, "the engine behind Kafka"): 14 alerts,
  4 page-outs, all false by construction.
- The schedule below was generated once, with the seed fixed beforehand. It was not re-drawn.

Known before the run, and it should be kept in mind when reading the result:

- The scenarios were ported by someone who knows the detectors. The guard against tailoring is that
  sizes are the batch scenarios' own constants (or what the batch physics produced on the train
  split), and every range is stated with its source in `sentinel_stream/scenarios_live.py`.
- Two scenarios restart a pod in their first minute because their batch originals do
  (`consumer_slow_deploy`: the rollout; `producer_crashloop`: the crash). A restart is a hard
  guardrail, so these are expected to page within a minute. A detection of those two says little.
- The two limitations found on the test week are what this world is mostly for: a signal on a series
  that sits at zero (`trino_queued_queries`, in `trino_heavy_query`), and a slow leak
  (`consumer_memory_leak`, `flink_state_leak`). Nothing was changed in the system to address them.
- No predictions are recorded: the run follows this commit directly, in the same working session.

## The system under test

The frozen final system, exactly as on the test week: the routed detectors, 3-of-5 persistence, the
correlator (15 min, 1 hop), the feedback groups frozen at the end of train, in the streaming engine
(`sentinel_stream/engine.py`, paging once per step). `sentinel_lens/` is identical to the tag
`pre-test-freeze`. The engine is warm-started on the 28 days before the stream and learns nothing
from the week.

The consumer and the engine never read the control or the answer-key topic
(`tests/test_live_isolation.py`).

## The world

- `data_live`: `python -m sentinel_sim.generate --no-incidents --days 35 --seed 11 --out data_live`.
- Stream: 2026-10-26 00:00 to 2026-11-02 00:00 UTC (7 days), published at max speed.
- Injections: the publisher changes the readings it sends from the injection time on
  (`sentinel_stream/injector.py`) and writes what it injected to the answer key.

## The schedule

Generated by `schedule()` in `scripts/run_live_week.py` with seed 1:

1. Every scenario twice (14 injections in 7 days), in a seeded random order.
2. For each, a target drawn from the scenario's targets and every parameter drawn uniformly from its
   range (durations and lead times in whole minutes, the rest rounded to 3 significant digits).
3. The first injection starts 60-240 minutes after the stream starts. Each next one starts 120 minutes
   plus a random extra of up to 720 minutes after the previous one stops, so injections never overlap
   and the scorer's 30-minute tail of one never reaches the next. A DAG pause starts `lead_min` before
   the next scheduled run of its DAG. If the week would overflow, all extras shrink by a common factor.

run if the schedule it generates is not this table.

## Scoring rule

Implemented in `sentinel_stream/live_score.py`, which reads only the answer key and the `pages` topic.

- A **page-out** is a `paged` event: the moment an engineer is paged. What the page holds at that
  moment is what counts.
- A page-out **matches** an injection when its time is inside [start, stop + 30 min] of the injection
  AND the page holds, at that moment, an alert on one of the injection's target entities or on an
  entity 1 hop from one of them (the correlator's entity graph).
- The **target entities** of an injection are the entities that own the series it changes, plus the
  DAG for a pause, as written to the answer key by the publisher. They are derived mechanically, not
  chosen per scenario.
- An injection is **detected** when at least one page-out matches it.
  **Time to detect** = time of its first matching page-out - its start.
- A page-out that matches no injection is **false**.

Two consequences of the rule, accepted in advance: an alert that joins a page after that page has
paged does not make a detection, and "1 hop from any series the injection touches" is generous for
scenarios that touch many entities (the crash loop touches the producer, the topic, the consumer
group, its pods and the Spark stream). The report names the alert each detection rests on, so a
reader can judge.

## What will be reported

In `docs/results/live-week-1.md`, written by the runner:

- Per injection: scenario, target, parameters, start, stop, detected or not, time to detect, the
  alert the first matching page-out rests on, the number of matching page-outs, and the number of
  alerts on or 1 hop from its targets inside its window (paged for or not).
- Overall: injections detected as a count "k of 14"; median and maximum time to detect over the
  detected ones; page-outs; false page-outs and false pages per day.
- By scenario: injected, detected, times to detect.
- Every false page-out, with the alerts its page held.
- For reference: the page-outs of the same week with nothing injected, and how many of the false
  page-outs here fall at the time of one of them.

The headline is two numbers: injections detected (k of 14) and false pages per day. With 14
injections the count carries a wide interval and will be described as a count, not a rate.

## Rules

1. **Run once.** `python scripts/run_live_week.py --run-once`. The runner refuses unless this file is
   committed, the code under `sentinel_lens/`, `sentinel_sim/`, `sentinel_stream/` and the runner
   itself are committed and unchanged, the generated schedule is the table above, and no result exists.
2. **Saved unedited.** The runner writes `docs/results/live-week-1.md` with the commit hash. The file
   is committed as written.
3. **If the run fails technically** (a crash, not a result), that is reported as such, the cause is
   fixed without touching scenarios, ranges, seed, rule or detectors, and the fix and the re-run are
   described in docs/log.md. Partial output of a failed run is not used.
4. **Anything later is post-hoc** and is labeled so: further runs, other seeds, other rules.

### What will NOT be done after seeing the numbers

- Change a scenario, a range, the seed, the schedule or the scoring rule and present the new number
  as the result.
- Change a detector, threshold, floor, persistence, the correlator or the feedback state and present
  that as the result. Fixes for what this week shows are future work, to be evaluated on a further
  world (another seed and schedule), not on this one.
- Drop an injection from the count because it looks unfair, or count a detection the rule does not give.
- Re-run with another seed and choose between runs.
- Explain a miss away. A missed injection is reported as missed, with what the system saw.

## Limits of this evaluation, stated beforehand

- The injected failures are declared effects, not physics: lag is a straight ramp, a crash loop sets
  readings to zero, a paused DAG's run is simply missing. Each port's approximations are listed with
  the scenario. A detection here is a detection of the port.
- One world, one seed, 14 injections, two per scenario.
- Logs and Kubernetes events are not produced, and the system does not read them.
- A consumer restart during the week would rebuild the engine from the data directory, which does not
  contain the injected readings. The run uses one uninterrupted consumer.
