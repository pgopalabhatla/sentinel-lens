## Alert score

- Default formula: severity. Guardrail detectors ('counter_rule', 'above_zero', 'start_late', 'sla_breach', 'failed_run') score 1.0; all others
  0.5 + 0.5 * (1 - exp(-(r - 1))), r = measure / alert line, kept in [0.5, 1). The score does not decide what fires or pages.

## Correlator

- `correlate` defaults: {'gap_min': 15, 'max_hops': 1}; applied to the raw alerts inside the evaluated window
- Topology: the relationships table plus these tracked extra edges (sentinel_lens/topology_extra.py):
  - resource_group:adhoc --member_of--> cluster:trino-lakehouse
  - spark_app:orders-stream --consumes--> topic:orders
  - flink_job:clickstream-enrich --consumes--> topic:clicks
  - dag:daily_orders --scheduled_by--> cluster:airflow-main
  - dag:customer_agg --scheduled_by--> cluster:airflow-main
  - dag:hourly_quality_check --scheduled_by--> cluster:airflow-main

## Feedback

- n_min 10, tau 0.25; estimate = (true + 1) / (labeled + 2)
- Labels: the scorer's verdicts on train alerts after its 30-minute dedup ('true' / 'false_positive'; 'expected' is not a label)
- FROZEN state for system (5): learned from ALL train labels (clock at the end of train), then applied unchanged
  to the whole evaluated period from its first alert. Nothing is learned from the evaluated period.
- Demoted groups carried into the test week: [('seasonal', 'cpu_throttling'), ('seasonal fallback', 'jvm_gc')]
- Exploratory online variant only: label delay 60 min, labels keep arriving during the test week
